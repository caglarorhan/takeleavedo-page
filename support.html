Frequently Asked Questions

1. Why am I not receiving arrival notifications?

Make sure the following prerequisites are met in Android System Settings:

  • Location Permission: Must be set to "Allow all the time" so Android can monitor geofences when the screen is locked or the app is closed.
  • Device Location: High accuracy device GPS must be switched ON.
  • Battery Optimization: Exclude TakeLeaveDo from aggressive OEM battery savers (e.g. Samsung Device Care, Xiaomi MIUI Battery Saver) so Android doesn't kill the geofence background receiver.
  • In-App Readiness Diagnostic: Tap the shield icon in the top toolbar inside the app to view the live diagnostic health of your signal providers.

2. How does the 2-Second Undo work for deleted items?

When you swipe an item or action card to the left, it transitions into a 2-second countdown state showing a progress bar and a bold UNDO button. If you accidentally swiped, simply tap UNDO to restore it immediately. After 2 seconds elapse without tapping Undo, the item is permanently deleted from the database.

3. What voice commands can I speak?

TakeLeaveDo features an advanced unpunctuated voice engine that extracts compound stops and actions in one sentence. For example:

"Create a stop at Harbor Freight on next Monday at 3 pm, collect drill set, deliver return invoice, and ask for manager"

The app will automatically extract Harbor Freight as the stop and place name, schedule it for the specified date and time, and categorize:

  • Take (Pick up): Drill set
  • Leave (Drop off): Return invoice
  • Do (Action): Ask for manager

4. How do I backup or transfer my stops to another phone?

Open the side drawer in TakeLeaveDo and tap Backup & Restore. Tap Export Full Backup to save an encrypted JSON file. On your new device, choose Import Backup to restore all your places, routines, and historical logs.
