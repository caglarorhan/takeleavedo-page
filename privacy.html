Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 4, 2026 • Version 1.0

Our Privacy Promise: TakeLeaveDo is built with privacy-first principles. Your routines, places, tasks, voice recordings, and GPS coordinates are processed and stored strictly on your device. We do not operate tracking servers, we do not require account registration, and we never sell, share, or monetize your personal data.

1. Overview & Data Architecture

TakeLeaveDo ("the App", "we", "our") is designed as an autonomous, on-device stop management and reminder utility. The app operates with an offline-first architecture using a local SQLite database (via Android Room). No cloud servers are required to operate the core features of the App.

2. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

The App requests specific Android system permissions to deliver its intended core functions:

2.1 Location Permissions (Foreground & Background)

Permissions: ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION

  • Purpose: To establish circular GPS geofences around your designated stops and places so that you receive automated arrival and departure reminders (e.g. reminding you what items to take or leave when reaching a job site).
  • Background Access Prominent Disclosure: TakeLeaveDo collects location data in the background even when the app is closed or not in use solely to trigger your automated arrival and departure checklist alerts.
  • On-Device Handling: All coordinate comparisons and geofence events are evaluated entirely on your phone via the Android Google Play Services Geofencing API. Your location coordinates are never transmitted over the internet or saved to external databases.

2.2 Wi-Fi & Nearby Device Access

Permissions: ACCESS_WIFI_STATE, CHANGE_WIFI_STATE, NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES

  • Purpose: To provide secondary presence corroboration indoors or in dense urban areas where GPS signals may drift. The app matches nearby Wi-Fi network hardware identifiers (BSSID) against places you have explicitly designated.
  • Handling: Wi-Fi scan evidence remains in your device's local memory and database. No network passwords or internet traffic from those Wi-Fi networks are accessed or recorded.

2.3 Audio & Microphone Access

Permissions: RECORD_AUDIO, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE

  • Purpose: To allow hands-free voice commands (such as creating stops, adding items, or dictating routine notes) and optional wake-word detection ("Hey TakeLeave").
  • Handling: Spoken audio is processed entirely on-device using local offline speech engines (Android SpeechRecognizer, Vosk, or on-device Whisper models). Audio samples are discarded immediately after transcription; your voice recordings are never stored as audio files or sent to remote cloud servers.

2.4 Camera Access

Permissions: CAMERA

  • Purpose: To scan QR codes containing fleet import URLs or routine configuration payloads.
  • Handling: The camera is accessed only while the QR scanner viewfinder dialog is visibly open. No photos or video frames are captured, stored, or uploaded.

2.5 Notifications

Permissions: POST_NOTIFICATIONS

  • Purpose: To post actionable arrival, departure, and reminder notifications when reaching scheduled stops.

4. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

The App performs network communication solely for user-initiated features:

  • OpenStreetMap / Nominatim: Used when searching for addresses or displaying map tiles over secure HTTPS. No personal identifying information is transmitted beyond the search query.
  • User-Configured JSON Import: If you scan a QR code or enter a custom URL to import a fleet schedule, the App downloads that JSON file directly from your designated server.
  • Optional AI Models: On-device voice and language models (e.g. Whisper, Gemma) are downloaded once directly from verified repositories (such as Hugging Face) over HTTPS and stored in local app storage.

5. User Data Control & Deletion

Because all data is stored exclusively on your device, you maintain 100% control over your information at all times:

  • Export Backup: You can export a full JSON backup of your places and flows at any time from the in-app Backup screen.
  • Revoke Permissions: You can revoke location, microphone, or camera permissions at any time via Android System Settings.
  • Complete Deletion: You can delete any place or stop directly within the app. Clearing app storage or uninstalling the app permanently purges all databases, cached audio, and diagnostic logs from your device.

6. Children's Privacy

TakeLeaveDo is intended for general audiences, couriers, and daily task management. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13.
